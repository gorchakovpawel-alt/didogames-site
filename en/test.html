The test is over

The closed test of The Last War: Train Defense is finished — thank you to everyone who played it and reported what broke. The game passed review and is published.

Where to play

On Android, on Google Play: the store page. On iPhone, on the App Store: the store page, iOS 17.0 or later. Nothing to join — just install it.

If you were a tester

Nothing to do: an installed test build updates to the public version on its own, and your progress stays. You can leave the tester group whenever you like — it no longer affects the game.

Questions

Something broken, or an idea — write to support@didogames.net. A human answers. Common questions are answered on the support page.
