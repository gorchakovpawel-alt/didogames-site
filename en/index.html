OVERVIEW

HARVEST FIELD // YOUR HANDS

Catch, don't aim

Crystals fall from the sky and have to be caught before they hit the ground. Miss them and the reactor stays cold, the tree stays locked.

PROGRESSION // SYSTEM RESTORE

Node tree

All in-run power lives in the restore circuit: 190+ nodes, paid in crystals between waves.

ROUTE // 10 BIOMES

From the Wastes to the Citadel

Ten biomes with their own enemies, bosses and weather — see the band below.

THE TRAIN // DEPOT

Fortress on rails

Platforms, wagons, guns, modules and trophy cores — build your own armored train.
